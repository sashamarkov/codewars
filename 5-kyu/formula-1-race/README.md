<h2><a href=https://www.codewars.com/kata/626d691649cb3c7acd63457b/train/python target="_blank">Formula 1 Race</a></h2><h3>5 kyu</h3><p>You are a big fan of Formula 1.</p><p>There was a race last week-end, but ... you missed the live TV.You picked on the internet a list of the <strong>events</strong> during the race, and you want to get your champion's rank at the end of the race.</p><p>An event is a pilot ID followed by a type. There are two types of events:</p><ul><li>'O' : a pilot is overtaking the one just in front of him</li><li>'I' : a pilot had an incident... and is forced to quit the race (and subsequently all pilots after this one gain one rank)</li></ul><p>The list of events is passed as a string where events are separated with a space, first event first. <em>e.g: "2 O 12 I" means the pilot#2 overtook, then the pilot#12 had an incident.</em></p><p>Your function will return the rank of your champion at the end of the race, given your champion's ID (as int) and the list of events. Return -1 if your champion had an incident.</p><p>NOTES:</p><ul><li>there are 20 positions when the race starts (from 1 to 20, 1 being the one in front),</li><li>therefore, the <strong>rank</strong> of a pilot, at start and during the race, is actually his/her <strong>position</strong> (first pilot at position 1, etc...),</li><li>each pilot ID is his position when the races starts,</li><li>all events in entry lists are syntactically valid.</li></ul><p><em>Final tests contains about 20 fixed events, and 100 random tests with race length between 10 to 30 events.In the tests (fixed and random): the leading pilot never overtakes, and after having an incident, the pilot never appears in further events.</em></p><p><strong>Enjoy !</strong></p><p><em>Thanks to the <strong>Best french developer Contest</strong> 2019</em>  </p>
