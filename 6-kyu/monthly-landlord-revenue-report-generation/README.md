The company oversees various properties, each owned by different landlords. Your task is to create a monthly report that shows how much each landlord should be paid. This payment is calculated by summing up the total rent from all properties owned by a landlord and then subtracting the total cost of maintenance carried out on those properties for the month.</p><p>Develop an SQL query that generates a monthly invoice for each landlord. The invoice should include the landlord's ID and name, total rent from all properties they own, total maintenance costs for those properties for a specific month (April, 2024), and the final amount payable to the landlord.</p><p>We have <code>landlord</code> table:</p><ul><li><code>id</code> (integer, primary Key): Unique identifier for the landlord.</li><li><code>name</code> (varchar): Name of the landlord.</li><li><code>email</code> (varchar): Email address of the landlord.</li></ul><p><code>property</code> table:</p><ul><li><code>id</code> (integer, primary Key): Unique identifier for the property.</li><li><code>address</code> (varchar): Physical address of the property.</li><li><code>rent</code> (integer): Monthly rent price for the property.</li><li><code>landlord_id</code> (integer, Foreign Key to <code>landlord</code> table): ID of the landlord who owns the property.</li></ul><p>and <code>maintenance</code> table:</p><ul><li><code>id</code> (integer, primary Key): Unique identifier for the maintenance record.</li><li><code>details</code> (varchar): Description of the maintenance work.</li><li><code>cost</code> (integer): Cost of the maintenance work.</li><li><code>maintenance_date</code> (date): Date when the maintenance was carried out.</li><li><code>property_id</code> (integer, foreign key to <code>property</code> table): ID of the property where the maintenance was performed.</li></ul><p>The query should output a table with columns:</p><ol><li><code>landlord_id</code> (integer): ID of the landlord.</li><li><code>name</code> (varchar): Name of the landlord.</li><li><code>total_rent</code> (integer): Total rent collected from all properties owned by the landlord.</li><li><code>total_cost</code> (integer): Total maintenance cost for those properties for the April, 2024.</li><li><code>amount_payable</code> (integer): The total rent minus the total maintenance cost.</li></ol><p>The final output should be ordered first by the <code>total_rent</code> in descending order, showcasing those landlords due the most at the top, and then by <code>landlord_id</code> in descending order for easy reference.</p><p>For this sample data:</p><pre><code>landlord:id  |    name    |       email       ----|------------|------------------- 1  | J Johnson  | jj@example.com     2  | R Kelly    | rk@example.com    </code></pre><pre><code>property:id |  address   | rent | landlord_id ---|------------|------|-------------1  | 123 Street | 400  |      1      2  | 456 Avenue | 600  |      1      3  | 789 Blvd   | 750  |      2     </code></pre><pre><code>maintenance: id  |   details      | cost | maintenance_date | property_id ----|----------------|------|------------------|------------1   | Fixing pipes   |  25  | 2024-04-05       |      1      2   | Painting walls | 120  | 2024-04-10       |      2      3   | Window replace |  35  | 2024-04-15       |      3      </code></pre><p> The desired output is
